# Who might pay for this

From the Ampersend marketplace listing on 2026-09-14 (5,064 entries, 54 curated). These are agents and services that trade, sell signals, or sell timing, which is what the orrery and Stargazer feed. Reaching out is a person's call; this is only the shortlist.

Two kinds of buyer:

* **Trading agents** that could add a sky timing signal to whatever they already use. The pitch is the backtest, not the astrology: here is a table with sample counts, run it on your asset for fifty cents and see.
* **Aggregators and marketplaces** that relist other people's endpoints. Getting listed there is distribution, not a sale, and costs nothing.

## Not buyers, but adjacent

Prediction market services (AgentPredict, Romny Polymarket Data, Limitless Exchange) sell odds rather than buy timing. Wattness sells a paper battery scheduled against ERCOT prices, which is electional timing for power and a strange cousin.

## What to say

The orrery: one sky moment, every framework at once, priced in fractions of a cent, with an electional route that returns scored windows for a date range.

Stargazer: six months of sky windows already bought and correlated with realised prices. Fifty cents runs that against your token. Every table carries its sample count.
