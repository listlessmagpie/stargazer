# Family 1: timing windows

Registered 2026-10-12, before the full inventory test is run.

**Question.** Does being in a market during a sky factor's active days beat being in it on the
other days, for the same market, over the same years?

**Inventory.** Every factor the sky ledger computes, with no pre selection: signs, phases,
retrogrades, stations, every aspect between transiting bodies, eclipse windows, and transits
to every chart in the library that belongs to the market (each aspect separately, every
transiting body including the Moon, the angles when the birth time is recorded, and the full
aspect set down to septiles, noviles, quintiles, antiscia and midpoints).

**Markets.** Bitcoin, Ethereum, S&P 500, gold, oil, VIX. Daily closes from 2005-01-01 or the
market's first day.

**Horizons.** 1 and 5 trading days ahead.

**Pass rule, unchanged from the September almanac.** Learned on days before 2021-01-01 with
|z| at or above 2.5; confirmed on days from 2021-01-01 with the same sign and |z| at or above
1.5; at least 60 active days and 8 separate episodes overall, 30 active days in the learning
years and 20 in the confirming years.

**Luck rule.** The whole study is rerun 100 times with the sky slid out of step with the
calendar by a random amount. The study as a whole has found something only if the real pass
count exceeds the 95th percentile of the shifted runs. A single factor is a candidate only if
the study as a whole passed, or if it is carried into family 2 as an occasion and passes there.

**What it earns.** A pass here earns a line on the free index and a forward log entry. It does
not earn pretend money by itself; the paper book takes family 2 occasions and the whole sky
forecast, not single window factors, because the fee arithmetic at the current stake rules
out many small trades.

**Expected result, stated now.** From the September run: the study as a whole indistinguishable
from chance, with a short list of survivors. The larger inventory raises the number of tests
and so the luck count; the pass count must rise with it or the result stands.
