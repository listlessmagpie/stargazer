# Family 2: occasions

Registered 2026-10-12.

**Question.** When a rare sky factor occurs, does the market move the way the record says, and
in how many of its separate occurrences?

**Unit.** The occurrence, not the day. Days inside one unbroken active stretch move together
and count once. A factor's record is "went as expected in N of M occurrences", with the split
before and after 2021-01-01 shown.

**Pass rule for the paper book.** At least 7 historical occurrences, at least 70% as expected
overall, and at least half of the occurrences since 2021 as expected. These numbers are set now
and are not tuned afterwards.

**Pass rule for live money.** The paper book rule, plus at least 10 forward occurrences on the
public log after registration, right more often than the market's own base rate over the same
days. Live wiring is Magpie's hand only.

**Position rule on the paper book.** Enter at the close of the first active day, hold for the
horizon or until the factor goes inactive, whichever is longer, one position per factor per
occurrence, equal pretend size. Short when the factor expects down, on venues that allow it
(Alpaca paper for stocks, ETFs and crypto; the Base book cannot short and sits out).

**Expected result, stated now.** Most candidates will fall below 70% as more occurrences arrive;
Venus retrograde and Venus opposite Jupiter are the two with a chance.
