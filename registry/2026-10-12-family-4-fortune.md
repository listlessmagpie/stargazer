# Family 4: the fortune question

Registered 2026-10-12, before any fortune data is collected.

**Question.** At each dated jump or fall in a library person's wealth or in the price of the
thing they run, what was transiting their chart, and does the same contact precede jumps for
other people in the library?

**Data.** Daily closes of the company a person runs or founded (the public proxy for their
fortune, since net worth series are not freely published day by day); dated events from
Forbes real time coverage where a free record exists; 13F and Form 4 filings for timing of
their own trades. Birth data only from Astro Databank with the Rodden rating recorded; a
person without a recorded time has no Moon, no angles and no houses in the test.

**Measure.** The person's chart transits as factors, against the 5 and 20 day return of their
company's stock or coin, with the same learn before 2021 and confirm after rule as family 1,
and the same luck test. Then the cross person test: a contact that passes for one person is
checked on every other person in the library; it counts across people only if it passes for at
least three with the same sign.

**Minimums.** At least 5 years of daily prices per person. Where the time is unknown, the
Moon and angles are left out of the inventory for that person rather than guessed.

**What it earns.** A line on the free index and a forward log. No money until it has passed
family 2's occasion rule on the forward log.

**Expected result, stated now.** Small samples, large claims in the practitioner literature,
little that survives. The value is in having the dated record either way.
