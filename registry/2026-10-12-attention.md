# Attention: demand on the shelf as a signal

Registered 2026-10-12, before the per instrument tally exists.

**Question.** Do the counts of agents reading, pricing and buying our line on an instrument
carry information about that instrument's next move or its volatility?

**Data.** The tally's per day, per instrument counts of found, priced and bought, kept as
counts only, never who. Requests for instruments not in the library are counted under the
request's own symbol and feed the library queue.

**Measure.** Each count, and its change from the prior week, as a factor against the 1 and 5
day return and absolute return of the instrument, with the family 1 pass rule and luck test
once there are at least 60 days of counts and at least 8 episodes above the median.

**Confounder, stated now.** Attention on our shelf tracks our own marketing and the registries'
traffic. Where a registry publishes its own traffic, our counts are read as a share of it.
Until then, any pass is marked "not separable from marketing" on the record.

**What it earns.** A line on the record. No money by itself.
